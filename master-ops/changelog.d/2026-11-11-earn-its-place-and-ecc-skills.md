A `hook` or `dispatch` row prints `exec` (not sized from disk) unless it carries `measured_tokens`, and ranks after every sized row; a card, skill, memory, or shared row with no file on disk prints `absent`. `load_ledger` rejects a malformed ledger (duplicate key, a non-enum `kind`/`loads`/`status`, or a `sources` list with a non-string entry) with exit 2 instead of crashing or silently passing it through. `--check` exits 1 naming each offender: a skill, registered hook, charter section, or `AGENTS.md` twin on disk with no ledger row; a non-numeric `measured_tokens`; an `untested` row with no string `plan`, or a non-grandfathered plan missing `owner` or a `measure_by` that does not match `YYYY-MM-DD`; a `safety` row with no `reason`; or a `helps`/`no-effect`/`hurts` row with no `sources`. Table cells escape `|` so a pipe in `plan`, `reason`, or `sources` cannot add a column. Regression test: `scripts/test-harness-inventory.sh`.
