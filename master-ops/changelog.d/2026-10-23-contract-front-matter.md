A contract may now open with a typed front matter block (`tier`, `runtime`, `repo` required; `budget`, `writes`, `standing_rules` optional), parsed and validated in `DispatchGate.check` before any Orca call. An unknown key or malformed line refuses with `CONTRACT_FRONT_MATTER`; a tier, runtime, or standing-rules mismatch refuses with `CONTRACT_TIER_MISMATCH`, `RUNTIME_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `CONTRACT_RUNTIME_MISMATCH`, or `STANDING_RULE_UNKNOWN`; a contract with no block only warns (`CONTRACT_FRONT_MATTER_MISSING`) this release. The parsed object is exposed on `GateDecision.front_matter` and in the CLI's JSON, so `master-ops/scripts/dispatch` reads named `standing_rules` blocks straight off the gate's own recorded check instead of re-parsing the contract, and appends each block's text (from the template-layer `master-ops/rules/standing-rules.md`, or the never-overwritten instance overlay `master-ops/config/standing-rules.overlay.md`) to the dispatched spec. Five canonical blocks ship: `headless`, `ack-token`, `placement`, `voice`, `pr-house`.
