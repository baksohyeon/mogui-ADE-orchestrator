`product-path-guard.sh` denied a `cd` target as unresolvable in two ordinary shapes: a redirection inside the same segment (`cd <dir> 2>/dev/null && ...`, which tokenizes the redirection operator off into its own tokens) and a command on the next physical line (`cd <dir>` followed by a newline then another command, since the tokenizer treated the newline as plain whitespace and merged both lines into one segment). Both now resolve: a redirection operator and its target are dropped from the `cd` operand count before counting, and a newline ends a segment the same way `;` does, so each line is checked on its own. A `cd` into a product root still denies, whether reached through a redirection-bearing segment or a later line, and a redirection target that itself resolves into a product root still denies with the existing redirection reason.
