Added declared and measured worker token budgets, provider usage readers, and the pre-dispatch Codex quota guard to the dispatch wrapper. Tier defaults use recent complete worker records with a minimum sample count and cache their input signature; Codex quota reads are scoped to the dispatch account and fail open when the account or snapshot is unknown. Settlement remains compatible with omitted runtime metadata, records pre-registration completions, and upgrades an earlier unmeasured result when a later measured result arrives.
