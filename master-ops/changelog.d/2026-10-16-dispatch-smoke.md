`master-ops/scripts/dispatch-smoke`, a new seat-run check, drives the real `scripts/dispatch` wrapper through one deterministic top-tier refusal and one real haiku worker under the `MOGUI_STATE_DIR` seam, and prints a one-line `smoke PASS`/`smoke FAIL layer=<layer> step=<step>` verdict naming the layer that broke: gate, orca, register, delivery, worker, or contract-read. It covers only the wrapper's created-pane path for `--runtime claude`; the reused-pane path, codex and cursor, and worker output quality stay out of scope. `master-ops/scripts/test-dispatch-smoke.sh` extracts the script's pure functions (the milestone parser and layer verdict, the isolation comparison, the expected-token derivation, and the precondition that refuses an unrendered wrapper) and runs them under CI with no Orca, no network, and no `claude`. `master-ops/docs/runbooks/dispatch-smoke.md` documents the localization table, exit codes, what `MOGUI_STATE_DIR` isolates and what stays shared by design, and the Orca field vocabulary measured for this contract.
